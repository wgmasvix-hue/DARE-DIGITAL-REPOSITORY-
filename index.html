Submit Your Work

DARE welcomes submissions of research papers, datasets, theses, and other scholarly content. Use the form below to begin the submission process, or login with your institutional credentials to continue.

Getting Started

Step 1: Prepare Your Content

Gather your document(s), metadata, and any supplementary materials. We accept PDF, Word, images, and data files.

Step 2: Create Account or Login

Sign in with your institutional account or ORCID. First time? Registration takes less than 2 minutes.

Step 3: Fill Submission Form

Provide title, abstract, authors, subject classification, and upload files. The process takes about 10-15 minutes.

Step 4: Review & Publish

Our team reviews your submission within 2-3 business days. Once approved, your work is published and discoverable globally.

Submission FAQs

What types of content can I submit?

You can submit research papers, theses, dissertations, datasets, books, conference proceedings, technical reports, and educational materials. All content types must be scholarly in nature.

Do I need permission to submit?

You must be the author or have explicit permission to distribute the work. For published materials, verify you hold appropriate rights or that the work is open access.

How long does review take?

Most submissions are reviewed within 2-3 business days. You'll receive email notifications at each stage: submission received, under review, and decision.

Can I edit my submission after upload?

Yes, you can edit metadata and upload corrected files before it's published. Once published, contact our support team for modifications.

About DARE

DARE (Digital Archive for Research & Education) is a global open-access repository platform built on DSpace technology. We're dedicated to making scholarly research and educational resources discoverable, accessible, and preserved for the future.

Founded in 2024, DARE serves researchers, educators, and institutions across the globe. Our mission is to democratize access to knowledge and accelerate the pace of research discovery through open, interoperable technology.

🎯

Our Mission

Advance open science and ensure lasting access to the world's scholarly record through sustainable, community-driven repository infrastructure.

🌍

Global Reach

We partner with institutions across continents to build a truly global research infrastructure that transcends borders.

🔓

Open Access

All content is freely accessible to everyone. No paywalls, no barriers—just open knowledge.

Built on Open Source

DARE is powered by DSpace, a robust, community-driven digital repository platform trusted by leading institutions worldwide. Our platform uses open standards and APIs for maximum interoperability.
