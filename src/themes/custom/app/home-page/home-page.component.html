Our Mission

An African knowledge commons, open to the world

DARE — the Digital Academic Repository & Education initiative — collects, preserves and shares African scholarship, research publications, datasets and educational resources. We centre African voices and epistemologies, keep provenance and licensing transparent, and make knowledge freely discoverable, citable and reusable for present and future generations.
